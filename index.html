Cave

A wine cellar inventory for iPhone and iPad. Keep track of the wine you store at home: what you have, where each bottle is, and when each vintage is suggested to be ready.

Free. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

[APP STORE LINK: added after release]

What it does

  • Homes, cellars, racks, rows and slots, with racks shown as grids of filled and empty places. Areas for bottles that are not on a rack.
  • A placement picker that suggests free places when you add or move bottles.
  • Barcode and wine label scanning. Labels are read on your device.
  • A photo of the label for each vintage, straightened on your device.
  • Suggested drink windows from a built-in table, with the states Hold, Ready, Past peak and Beyond keeping. You can edit any window.
  • A journal of bottles taken out, with the occasion, a rating and a tasting note.
  • iCloud sync between your iPhone and iPad, and sharing your cellars with people you invite.
  • CSV export and CellarTracker CSV import.
  • English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Arabic.

Cave is an inventory tool for the wine you already own. It does not sell alcohol.
